How it works

Hatch your bee

Install a small program and choose when your PC is free, for example at night.

It works, you rest

It completes small cells of work. As soon as you come back to your PC, it steps aside.

Spend your honey

Use honey to have the hive clean, count and organise your texts.

Your data stays safe

Before anything leaves your PC, your texts are scrubbed: email addresses, phone numbers, IBANs and Italian tax codes are removed. Other bees only receive small encrypted, shuffled pieces and never learn who they come from. Once the job is done, the originals are deleted.

For now the hive only accepts non-sensitive texts. Read how we protect your data.

How we protect your data, in plain words

      
    
        
  1. Scrubbed on your PC. Before anything is sent, your browser removes email addresses, phone numbers, IBANs and Italian tax codes. Personal names are not detected, which is why the hive only accepts non-sensitive texts.
    2. 
        
  2. Small, shuffled pieces. The job is split into many small cells, mixed with other people's cells and without your name.
    3. 
        
  3. Always encrypted. Cells travel, and sit on other bees' disks, encrypted. The key only arrives at the moment of computation and is destroyed straight afterwards.
    4. 
        
  4. A closed pen. The computation runs in a sandbox that cannot read files, use the internet or display anything.
    5. 
        
  5. Surprise checks. Some cells are redone by a different bee to catch cheaters. You can also run a "spot check" on your own results.
    6. 
        
  6. Originals deleted. Your original texts stay on the server only while the job is running (7 days at most) and are then deleted.
    7. 
        
  7. What is kept. The hive keeps the processed results so it never redoes work already done. No other user can read them: search only shows the texts of your own jobs. You can ask for them to be deleted at any time.
    8. 
        
  8. The bee doing the work can see its piece. The bee processing a cell has to read it to compute it. That is why, for now, the hive only accepts non-sensitive texts.
    9. 
      

      
An honest limit: a very skilled person could, in theory, peek at a single piece while it is being computed on their own PC. That is why every piece is tiny, shuffled and already scrubbed.
